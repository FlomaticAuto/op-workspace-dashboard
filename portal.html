Score Methodology
Metric Weight What it measures
Purchase Frequency 25% Orders per month over 3 / 6 / 12-month windows
Basket Coverage 30% % of 11 product categories purchased (weighted to 12m)
Rep Visit Cadence 15% Visits per month from Zoho, matched by store name
SKU Depth 15% Within purchased categories, % of available SKUs bought
Rand Value Trend 15% 6-month linear trend in monthly spend (normalised slope)
🟢 Growing ≥ 65 🟡 Narrow 45–64 🟠 At-Risk 25–44 🔴 Dormant < 25
