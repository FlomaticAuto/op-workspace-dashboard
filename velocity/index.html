Revenue Velocity · How to Read This Report
Are Your Stores Speeding Up or Slowing Down?
Velocity shows whether an account is growing faster or slower this year compared to the same period last year — not just whether sales are up or down. A store buying R200K this year is a very different signal if last year it was R100K versus R400K.

1
Same window, three years.
We only look at this period of data — the same calendar months in FY2024, FY2025, and FY2026. This keeps the comparison fair even though FY2026 is not complete yet.
2
Two YoY growth rates.
FY25 rate = how much a store grew in this period FY25 vs FY24.
FY26 rate = how much it grew in this period FY26 vs FY25.
3
Velocity = FY26 rate minus FY25 rate.
Positive = accelerating (buying more aggressively than this time last year).
Negative = decelerating (slowing down, even if still growing).
Formula Velocity (ppts) = [ (FY26 ÷ FY25 − 1) − (FY25 ÷ FY24 − 1) ] × 100
