- Non-traditional paint stores (NTPS) and supermarkets in township areas are underpenetrated

## Market Conditions

### Pricing Pressure
- 2026-01 CEO Report: "Nobody is selling close to +15%, which is our target." Indicates market-wide pricing pressure, not isolated to specific reps.
- The 2026 15% list price increase appears to have been largely offset by rep discounting — particularly on High Gloss.

### Volume Dynamics
- High Gloss is the dominant volume driver. Its low rock bottom performance (+1.75%) pulls down the company-wide RB average significantly.
- Key volume categories identified in CEO report: High Gloss, Decor, Rain-proof — all pulling down the overall rock bottom percentage.
