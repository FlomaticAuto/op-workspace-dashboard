## 2026 List Price Increase — Effectiveness

The 2026 list price increase of 15% was applied across all enamel tiers. However, average actual selling prices show reps are discounting 20–34% below list, suggesting the increase was substantially offset by rep concessions. Assessment:

- **High Gloss:** 33% average discount vs list. Likely the 15% increase was not retained — reps reverted to near-prior pricing. The increase may have created no net revenue benefit.
- **Ultimate Shine:** 20–27% average discount. Premium positioning not being held at store level.
- **Pick 'N Save:** 25–34% discount. Thin margin maintained only because rock bottom is significantly below list.

---

## Pricing Rules & Governance

- **Rock bottom = absolute floor.** No rep may sell below rock bottom without written management sign-off.
- **Rep target = +15% above rock bottom.** No enamel group is currently meeting this.
- **Deep discounts require escalation.** Any transaction below rock bottom should auto-flag on the rep KPI dashboard.
- **Contract pricing:** Where large customers require structural discounts (contractors, bulk buyers), a signed tiered pricing agreement should replace ad-hoc rep discretion.
