## Customer Segment Intelligence

### Contractors & Builders
- Natural home for **Ultimate Shine** premium positioning — value quality over price
- Byron Minnie's primary channel
- Should not be offered ad-hoc discounts; use signed tiered contract pricing if bulk discount is required

### Hardware Stores (Traditional)
- Main channel for **High Gloss** mid-range volume
- Heavy price negotiation culture — rep discount discipline critical here
- Eclipse brand is a known competitor stocked at some accounts (e.g. GG 104)

### Non-Traditional Paint Stores (NTPS) / Supermarkets
- **Pick 'N Save** is the right entry product
- COD terms often required (Thandabunga Supermarket confirmed)
- Kagiso, Lenasia, Mabopane are active geographic areas

### Interior Designers / Specifiers
- Emerging channel — Homemakers show contact (Sahara Michelle Interiors)
- Ultimate Shine positioning fits this segment
