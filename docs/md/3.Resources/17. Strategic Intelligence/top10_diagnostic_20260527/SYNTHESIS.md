## Three patterns that cut across stores

**1. PVA is being displaced — distinguish mix-shift from real loss.** PVA *share* fell in 8 of 10 accounts FY24→FY26 (Patel −25pp, Builders −15pp, Dada −13pp, Kit Kat −12pp, HGM −7pp). But absolute PVA revenue (2025Q1→2026Q1, like-for-like quarters) splits two ways:
   - **Real collapse:** Brits (R12k→R2k, −84%), Dada (R156k→R39k, −75%), Patel (R159k→R47k, −70%), Builders (R150k→R87k, −42%).
   - **Still growing:** Kit Kat (+117%), Easy Build (+46%), HGM (+44%), Dainty (+33%).
   Patel is the instructive case: PVA fell 70% while Accessories (+16pp) and QD Enamel (+11pp) share rose — a deliberate, healthy basket recomposition, not a lost account. Crest Acrylic PVA prices ~61% below Olympic (and Continental in the NP corridor) make PVA the category where price gaps bite hardest, so the collapses at Brits/Dada warrant a PVA-specific defence.

**2. A financial-distress cluster, not a competitor cluster.** The two clearest declines after the bulk-order pair — Dada (−18%) and Dainty (−26%) — are both carrying extreme aged debt (92% and 84% of balance overdue 60d+). These read as customers in cash-flow distress reducing orders, not shelf share lost to a rival. Collections, not pricing, is the lever.

**3. Below-rock-bottom selling is endemic but on thin samples.** Where rock-bottom prices exist, 50–100% of those lines sold below floor in the latest quarter (HGM 100%, Dada 82%, Dainty 76%, Builders 71%, Brits 67%). Only ~14% of lines carry an RB reference, so treat as directional — but the direction is one-way: discounting is being used defensively and is not arresting the declines.

**4. The crown-jewel accounts have no competitor intelligence.** Not one of the 10 has competitor presence logged in Rep_Account_Health; visit notes carry zero competitor brand mentions for them. We are diagnosing 39% of revenue with the competitor column empty. Where a decline is competitor-driven (Brits/Dada PVA collapse) we are inferring, not observing. Mandating a 'competitors on shelf' field on every top-account visit would convert these inferences into evidence within one call cycle.
