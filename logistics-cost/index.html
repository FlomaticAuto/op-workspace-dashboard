Caveat

RMZ Freight invoices cover ALL courier shipments (e-commerce + wholesale rep deliveries), but the revenue denominator is WooCommerce-only. This inflates the ratio. Pudo Wallet costs are e-commerce-only and a cleaner signal. To improve the metric, tag RMZ line items by destination channel.

How this is computed
  • Costs from ecommerce_logistics_cost.parquet (RMZ Freight PDFs + Pudo Wallet CSVs).
  • Revenue from Woocommerce_Transactions.csv, deduped by order_id, summed by date_paid month.
  • Both sides incl-VAT. Months without invoices show 0% — pre-Nov 2025 invoices have not been ingested.
